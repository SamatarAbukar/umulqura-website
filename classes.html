Course Syllabus

Qa'idah Noorani & Tajweed Course

A seven-level journey — from recognising the letters to reciting with full Tajweed. Here is what a student masters at each milestone along the way.

  1. 1

    Level One

    Reading the letters

    Recognise every Arabic letter and read it correctly with the short vowels.

  2. 2

    Level Two

    Building whole words

    Apply Tanween and Tashdeed, and produce the nasal sound of Ghunna while reading.

  3. 3

    Level Three

    Length and stopping

    Hold each Madd for its proper length, and stop correctly at the end of a verse.

  4. 4

    Level Four

    The first Tajweed rules

    Learn the vocabulary of Tajweed and the complete rules of Izhaar and Idghaam.

  5. 5

    Level Five

    Hiding, echoing, stopping

    Complete Qalb, Ikhfa and Qalqalah, along with the full rules of Waqf.

  6. 6

    Level Six

    Mastering the Makhaarij

    Finish every articulation point of the letters, all types of Madd, and the signs of Waqf.

  7. 7

    Level Seven

    Revision & certification

    Revise every rule and the Makhaarij daily, then sit the final test to earn the certificate.
